# news_nvidia_nvlink_fusion_semi_custom_ai_infrastructure_partner_ecosystem.html

NVIDIA press release (May 18, 2025) unveiling NVIDIA NVLink Fusion — new silicon that enables industries to build semi-custom AI infrastructure with the NVIDIA NVLink partner ecosystem. Announced at COMPUTEX 2025, the release covers partner adoptions from MediaTek, Marvell, Alchip, Astera Labs, Synopsys, Cadence, Fujitsu, and Qualcomm; mentions GB200 NVL72 and GB300 NVL72 rack-scale systems; NVIDIA Mission Control software; and NVIDIA networking products including ConnectX-8 SuperNICs, Spectrum-X, and Quantum-X800 InfiniBand.

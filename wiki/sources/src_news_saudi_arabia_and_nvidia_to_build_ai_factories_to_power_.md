# news_saudi_arabia_and_nvidia_to_build_ai_factories_to_power_next_wave_of_intelligence_for_the_age_of_reasoning.html

NVIDIA and Saudi Arabia announced a partnership to build AI factories and sovereign AI infrastructure in the Kingdom. HUMAIN (PIF subsidiary) will deploy up to 500 MW of GPU capacity including an 18,000 GB300 supercomputer. SDAIA will deploy 5,000 Blackwell GPUs for sovereign AI and smart cities. Aramco Digital will develop AI computing infrastructure and a robotics center of excellence. The initiative aligns with Saudi Vision 2030 and includes training, Omniverse Cloud, and digital twin solutions for energy, manufacturing, and logistics.

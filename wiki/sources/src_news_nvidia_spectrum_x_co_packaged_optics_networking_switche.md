# news_nvidia_spectrum_x_co_packaged_optics_networking_switches_ai_factories.html

NVIDIA press release from March 18, 2025 (GTC) announcing NVIDIA Spectrum-X Photonics and NVIDIA Quantum-X Photonics silicon photonics networking switches. These co-packaged optics switches enable AI factories to connect millions of GPUs across sites while drastically reducing energy consumption and operational costs. Key specs include 1.6 Tbps per port, 3.5x energy savings, 10x resilience, 4x fewer lasers, 63x greater signal integrity. Spectrum-X Photonics Ethernet switches (100-400Tb/s) expected in 2026; Quantum-X Photonics InfiniBand switches (144 ports of 800Gb/s) expected later in 2025. Partners include TSMC, Coherent, Corning, Foxconn, Lumentum, SENKO, and others.

# news_nvidia_announces_financial_results_for_first_quarter_fiscal_2026.html

NVIDIA's official Q1 FY2026 earnings announcement (quarter ended April 27, 2025). The company reported revenue of $44.1 billion, up 12% Q/Q and 69% Y/Y. Key highlights include: $4.5 billion H20 inventory charge due to new U.S. export licensing requirements for China; Blackwell platform in full-scale production; Data Center revenue of $39.1 billion; record Gaming revenue of $3.8 billion; partnerships with HUMAIN, Foxconn, G42, Alphabet/Google, Oracle, Microsoft, AWS, and Nintendo; launches of Blackwell Ultra, Dynamo, DGX Spark, DGX Station, RTX PRO, DLSS 4, Omniverse, Isaac GR00T N1/N1.5, Cosmos, and Llama Nemotron. Q2 FY2026 revenue outlook is $45.0 billion.

# news_foxconn_ai_factory_tsmc_taiwan_nvidia.html

NVIDIA press release dated May 18, 2025 announcing that Foxconn (Hon Hai Technology Group) is deepening its partnership with NVIDIA and the Taiwan government to build an AI factory supercomputer. The system, built through Foxconn subsidiary Big Innovation Company as an NVIDIA Cloud Partner, will feature 10,000 NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs and NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra systems including GB300 NVL72. TSMC will leverage the system for R&D, and the Taiwan National Science and Technology Council will invest to provide AI cloud computing resources to Taiwan's technology ecosystem. The system will use NVIDIA NVLink, Quantum InfiniBand, and Spectrum-X networking, and will participate in the NVIDIA DGX Cloud Lepton marketplace. It will also support NVIDIA Inception and Deep Learning Institute programs. Foxconn plans to use the AI supercomputer to accelerate smart cities, electric vehicles, and manufacturing applications.

# NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf

NVIDIA Investor Presentation for Q4 FY26 (quarter ended January 2026), published February 2026. The presentation covers NVIDIA's record quarterly results including: revenue of $68.1B (up 73% YoY), Data Center Compute revenue of $51.3B, Data Center Networking revenue of $11.0B, GAAP gross margin of 75.0%, GAAP operating income of $44.3B, GAAP net income of $43.0B, GAAP diluted EPS of $1.76, and free cash flow of $34.9B. The presentation also provides Q1 FY27 outlook ($78.0B revenue, 75.0% non-GAAP gross margin, $7.5B non-GAAP operating expenses), forward-looking statements, and detailed GAAP to non-GAAP reconciliations. Key products discussed include Blackwell, Blackwell Ultra, NVLink, Spectrum-X, InfiniBand, Hopper, and Ampere.

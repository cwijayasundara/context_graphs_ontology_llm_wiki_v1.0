# Stock-Based Compensation

NVIDIA's stock-based compensation expense. A foundational component of compensation program to attract and retain world-class talent. Beginning in Q1 FY27, NVIDIA will include stock-based compensation expense in its non-GAAP financial measures. Q1 FY27 SBC expense expected to be $1.9 billion, with 0.1% impact on non-GAAP gross margin.

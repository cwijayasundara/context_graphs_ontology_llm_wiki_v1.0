# NVIDIA Cloud Partner

NVIDIA Cloud Partner is a program where partners provide cloud-based NVIDIA GPU infrastructure to customers. In this press release dated May 18, 2025, Foxconn's subsidiary Big Innovation Company is announced as an NVIDIA Cloud Partner, providing AI infrastructure through the Big Innovation Cloud AI factory featuring 10,000 NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs.

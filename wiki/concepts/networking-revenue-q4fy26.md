# Networking Revenue Q4 FY26

NVIDIA's Networking revenue for Q4 FY26 was a record $10,980 million ($11.0 billion), up 263% from a year ago (Q4 FY25: $3,024M) and up 34% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $8,187M). Growth driven by the introduction and continued ramp of NVLink compute fabric for GB200 and GB300 systems and the growth of Ethernet and InfiniBand platforms.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Revenue by Market Platform table (Q4).

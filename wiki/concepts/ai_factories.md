# AI Factories

AI factories are large-scale AI infrastructure facilities designed to power advanced computing workloads. In the Saudi Arabia-NVIDIA partnership, HUMAIN is investing in AI factories with a projected capacity of up to 500 megawatts powered by several hundred thousand NVIDIA GPUs over five years. The first phase includes an 18,000 NVIDIA GB300 Grace Blackwell AI supercomputer with InfiniBand networking.

# NVIDIA Q1 FY27 Outlook Non-GAAP Gross Margin

NVIDIA Q1 FY27 Non-GAAP Gross Margin outlook is **75.0%** (+/- 50 bps). The corresponding GAAP gross margin is expected to be 74.9%. Starting in Q1 FY27, stock-based compensation (SBC) expense will be included in non-GAAP results. SBC is expected to have a 0.1% impact on non-GAAP gross margin. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, Q1 FY27 Outlook slide (page 10) and reconciliation table (page 14).

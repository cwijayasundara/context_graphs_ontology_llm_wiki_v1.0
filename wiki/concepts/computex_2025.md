# COMPUTEX 2025

COMPUTEX 2025 is a major international technology trade show held in Taipei, Taiwan. NVIDIA unveiled NVIDIA NVLink Fusion at COMPUTEX 2025, with Jensen Huang delivering a keynote address. The event served as the launch platform for NVIDIA's new silicon enabling semi-custom AI infrastructure.

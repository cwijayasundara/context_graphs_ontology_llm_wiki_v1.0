# Q4 FY26 GAAP Effective Tax Rate

NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 GAAP effective tax rate was 14.8%, an increase from a year ago primarily due to a lower impact from stock-based compensation tax benefits.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Other Income & Expense and Income Tax section.

NVIDIA Data Center Compute revenue was **$51.3 billion** in Q4 FY26, up from $32.6 billion in Q4 FY25. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, Data Center revenue breakdown table (page 3).

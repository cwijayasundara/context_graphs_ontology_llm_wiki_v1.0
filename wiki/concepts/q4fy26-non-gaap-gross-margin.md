# Q4 FY26 Non-GAAP Gross Margin

NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 Non-GAAP gross margin was 75.2%, up from 73.6% in Q3 FY26 and 73.5% in Q4 FY25. The increase from a year ago was driven by lower inventory provisions. The sequential increase was driven by Blackwell ramp with improved mix and cost structure.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Non-GAAP summary table.

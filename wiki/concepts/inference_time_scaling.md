# Inference Time Scaling

Inference time scaling is the practice of scaling compute resources during the inference (deployment) phase of AI to improve model performance and reasoning capabilities. Jensen Huang highlighted that companies are racing to build AI factories that can scale to meet the processing demands of reasoning AI and inference time scaling.

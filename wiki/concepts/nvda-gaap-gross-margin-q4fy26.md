# NVIDIA GAAP Gross Margin Q4 FY26

NVIDIA GAAP Gross Margin was **75.0%** in Q4 FY26, up 2.0 percentage points from 73.0% in Q4 FY25. The year-on-year increase was driven by lower inventory provisions. The sequential increase was driven by the continued ramp of Blackwell with an improved mix and cost structure. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, GAAP P&L table and Gross Margin table.

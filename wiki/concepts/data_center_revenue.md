# Data Center Revenue

Data Center Revenue represents the portion of NVIDIA's revenue derived from sales of data center products and services, including AI accelerators (such as the H100 and Blackwell architectures), networking solutions, and cloud infrastructure platforms.

Per the Q4 FY26 quarterly revenue trend document, Data Center revenue grew from $22,563M in Q1 FY25 to $62,314M in Q4 FY26, making it NVIDIA's largest and fastest-growing market segment.

Quarterly figures ($ in millions):
- Q4 FY26: $62,314
- Q3 FY26: $51,215
- Q2 FY26: $41,096
- Q1 FY26: $39,112
- Q4 FY25: $35,580
- Q3 FY25: $30,771
- Q2 FY25: $26,272
- Q1 FY25: $22,563

# Data Center Revenue

Data Center revenue is NVIDIA's largest revenue segment. According to the Q4 FY26 investor presentation, total Data Center revenue reached $62.3B in Q4 FY26, consisting of Compute revenue of $51.3B and Networking revenue of $11.0B. This compares to Q4 FY25 Data Center revenue of $35.6B (Compute $32.6B + Networking $3.0B). Data Center revenue is up nearly 13x since the emergence of ChatGPT. The growth was driven by sustained strength in Blackwell and Blackwell Ultra for Compute, and strong adoption of NVLink, Spectrum-X, and InfiniBand for Networking.

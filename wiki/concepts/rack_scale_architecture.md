# Rack-Scale Architecture

Rack-scale architecture refers to computing systems designed at the scale of an entire server rack rather than individual servers. NVIDIA's rack-scale solutions include GB200 NVL72 and GB300 NVL72 compute-dense racks that provide a total bandwidth of 1.8 TB/s per GPU. NVLink Fusion enables partners like Fujitsu and Qualcomm to couple their custom CPUs with NVIDIA GPUs in a rack-scale architecture to boost AI performance. Hyperscalers can standardize their heterogeneous silicon data centers on the NVIDIA rack architecture with NVLink Fusion.

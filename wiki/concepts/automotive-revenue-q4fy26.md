# Automotive Revenue Q4 FY26

NVIDIA's Automotive revenue for Q4 FY26 was $604 million, up 6% from a year ago (Q4 FY25: $570M) and up 2% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $592M). Driven by continued adoption of self-driving platforms.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Revenue by Market Platform table (Q4).

# FY26 Non-GAAP Gross Margin

NVIDIA's FY26 Non-GAAP gross margin was 71.3%, down from 75.5% in FY25 (a decrease of 4.2 percentage points).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Fiscal 2026 Non-GAAP summary table.

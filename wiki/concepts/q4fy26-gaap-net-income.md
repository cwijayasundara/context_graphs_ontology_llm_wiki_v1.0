# Q4 FY26 GAAP Net Income

NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 GAAP net income was $42,960 million ($43.0 billion), up 35% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $31,910M) and up 94% year-over-year (Q4 FY25: $22,091M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, GAAP summary table.

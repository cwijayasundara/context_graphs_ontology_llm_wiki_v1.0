# AI Infrastructure / AI Factories

AI infrastructure and AI factories are a cornerstone of the NVIDIA-Google Cloud collaboration. Google Cloud will be among the first to adopt NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 and RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition. Google Cloud offers A4 (B200-based) and A4X (GB200-based) VMs as the first cloud provider with both instance types. The collaboration also includes optimizing frameworks like JAX and MaxText for efficient training on tens of thousands of NVIDIA GPUs. Blackwell Ultra increases revenue opportunity by 50x for AI factories compared with Hopper-based ones.

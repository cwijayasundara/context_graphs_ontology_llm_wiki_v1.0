# Capital Return

Capital return represents NVIDIA's program to return cash to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases. According to the Q4 FY26 investor presentation, capital return in Q4 FY26 consisted of $15.5B in share repurchases and $8.1B in dividends, for total capital return of $23.6B. This compares to Q4 FY25 capital return of $4.1B. The presentation notes that NVIDIA is 'investing for growth while returning capital to shareholders.'

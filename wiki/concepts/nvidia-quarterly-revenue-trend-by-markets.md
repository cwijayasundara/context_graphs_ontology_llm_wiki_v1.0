# NVIDIA Quarterly Revenue Trend by Markets

NVIDIA Quarterly Revenue Trend by Markets is a financial reporting table showing NVIDIA's revenue broken down by market segment (Data Center, Gaming, Professional Visualization, Auto, and OEM & Other) across eight quarters from Q1 FY25 through Q4 FY26. All figures are in millions of U.S. dollars. The table shows consistent growth in total revenue from $26,044M in Q1 FY25 to $68,127M in Q4 FY26, driven primarily by the Data Center segment.

# Non-GAAP Stock-Based Compensation Policy Change FY27

Beginning in the first quarter of fiscal 2027, NVIDIA will include stock-based compensation expense in its non-GAAP financial measures. Stock-based compensation is described as a foundational component of NVIDIA's compensation program to attract and retain world-class talent.

For Q1 FY27, stock-based compensation expense is expected to have a 0.1% impact on non-GAAP gross margin and $1.9 billion in non-GAAP operating expenses.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Outlook section and GAAP to Non-GAAP Outlook reconciliation table.

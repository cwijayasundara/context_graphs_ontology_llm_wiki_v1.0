# Q4 FY26 Free Cash Flow

NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 free cash flow was $34,902 million, calculated as GAAP net cash provided by operating activities ($36,190M) less purchases related to property and equipment and intangible assets ($(1,284)M) and principal payments on property and equipment and intangible assets ($(4)M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Reconciliation of GAAP to Non-GAAP Financial Measures table.

# NVIDIA DGX Cloud Lepton

NVIDIA DGX Cloud Lepton is a marketplace connecting developers to NVIDIA's global compute ecosystem. In this press release dated May 18, 2025, Big Innovation Cloud (Foxconn's subsidiary) plans to participate in the NVIDIA DGX Cloud Lepton marketplace. This will provide enterprises — from startups and research institutions to established industry leaders — easy access to advanced GPU resources, further accelerating AI development and deployment in Taiwan.

# Semi-Custom AI Infrastructure

Semi-custom AI infrastructure refers to data center architectures that combine standardized AI computing platforms (such as NVIDIA GPUs and NVLink) with custom silicon and components from partner companies. NVIDIA NVLink Fusion enables this approach by allowing industries to build specialized AI infrastructures that integrate custom ASICs, CPUs, and interconnects while leveraging NVIDIA's AI platform and ecosystem. This approach provides flexibility for meeting demanding workloads for model training and agentic AI inference.

# NVIDIA GAAP Gross Margin Dollars Q4 FY25

NVIDIA GAAP Gross Margin in dollars was **$28,723 million** ($28.723 billion) in Q4 FY25, reconciled from Non-GAAP gross margin of $28,894 million. Adjustments: $(118) million acquisition-related costs, $(53) million stock-based compensation. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, Q4 FY25 reconciliation table (page 13).

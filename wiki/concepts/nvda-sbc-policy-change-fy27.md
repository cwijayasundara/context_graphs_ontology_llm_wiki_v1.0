# NVIDIA Stock-Based Compensation Policy Change FY27

Starting in Q1 FY27, NVIDIA will include stock-based compensation (SBC) expense in non-GAAP results. SBC is described as a foundational component of NVIDIA's compensation program to attract and retain world-class talent. For Q1 FY27, SBC is expected to have a 0.1% impact on non-GAAP gross margin and $1.9 billion impact on non-GAAP operating expenses. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, Q1 FY27 Outlook slide (page 10) and reconciliation table (page 14).

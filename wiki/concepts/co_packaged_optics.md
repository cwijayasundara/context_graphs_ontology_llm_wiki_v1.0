# Co-Packaged Optics

Co-packaged optics (CPO) refers to the integration of optical transceivers directly into switch packages rather than using pluggable transceivers. NVIDIA's Spectrum-X Photonics and Quantum-X Photonics switches integrate silicon photonics directly into the switch, shattering the old limitations of hyperscale and enterprise networks and opening the gate to million-GPU AI factories.

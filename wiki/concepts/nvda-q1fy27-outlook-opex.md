# NVIDIA Q1 FY27 Outlook Non-GAAP Operating Expenses

NVIDIA Q1 FY27 Non-GAAP Operating Expenses outlook is approximately **$7.5 billion**. The corresponding GAAP operating expenses are expected to be $7.7 billion. Starting in Q1 FY27, stock-based compensation (SBC) expense will be included in non-GAAP results. SBC expense for Q1 FY27 is expected to be $1.9 billion in non-GAAP operating expenses. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, Q1 FY27 Outlook slide (page 10) and reconciliation table (page 14).

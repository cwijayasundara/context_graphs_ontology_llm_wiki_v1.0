# Q4 FY26 GAAP Cost of Revenue

NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 GAAP cost of revenue was $17,034 million. Non-GAAP cost of revenue was $16,918 million after adjusting for acquisition-related and other costs ($48M), stock-based compensation expense ($69M), and other items ($(1)M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Reconciliation of GAAP to Non-GAAP Financial Measures table.

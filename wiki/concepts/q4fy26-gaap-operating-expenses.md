# Q4 FY26 GAAP Operating Expenses

NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 GAAP operating expenses were $6,794 million, up 45% from a year ago (Q4 FY25: $4,689M) and up 16% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $5,839M). Increases were driven by higher compensation and benefits expense due to employee growth, and compute and infrastructure costs. Sequential increase was driven by engineering development materials for new product introductions and compute and infrastructure costs.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, GAAP summary table and Expenses section.

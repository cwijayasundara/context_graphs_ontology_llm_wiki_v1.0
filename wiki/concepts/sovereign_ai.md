# Sovereign AI

Sovereign AI refers to AI infrastructure and expertise owned and controlled by a nation. NVIDIA and Saudi Arabia are harnessing sovereign AI infrastructure to propel the Kingdom to the ranks of global hyperscale AI leaders. SDAIA will deploy up to 5,000 Blackwell GPUs for a sovereign AI factory and enable smart city solutions.

# Gaming Market Platform

NVIDIA's Gaming market platform. Q4 FY26 revenue was $3.7 billion, up 47% YoY but down 13% sequentially. FY26 revenue was $16.0 billion, up 41% YoY. Q4 growth driven by strong Blackwell demand. Sequential decline due to channel inventory moderation after strong holiday demand. Supply constraints expected to be a headwind in Q1 FY27 and beyond.

# NVIDIA Data Center Networking Revenue Q4 FY26

NVIDIA Data Center Networking revenue was **$11.0 billion** in Q4 FY26, up from $3.0 billion in Q4 FY25. NVIDIA is described as the world's largest networking business. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, Data Center revenue breakdown table (page 3).

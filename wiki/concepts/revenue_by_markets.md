# Revenue by Markets

Revenue by Markets is a financial reporting approach that breaks down total company revenue into distinct market segments or business units. This segmentation helps analysts understand which markets are driving growth and which may be underperforming.

NVIDIA reports revenue across five primary market segments: Data Center, Gaming, Professional Visualization, Auto, and OEM & Other.

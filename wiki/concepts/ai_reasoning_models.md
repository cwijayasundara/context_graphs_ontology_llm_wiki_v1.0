# AI reasoning models

AI reasoning models are AI models designed to "think" and generate many tokens in response to prompts, using intermediate reasoning steps. As AI reasoning goes mainstream, every AI model will generate tens of thousands of tokens used to "think" with every prompt. NVIDIA Dynamo is specifically designed to accelerate and scale AI reasoning models in AI factories at the lowest cost and highest efficiency. Examples mentioned include DeepSeek-R1 and the NVIDIA Llama Nemotron model family.

# Q1 FY27 GAAP Operating Expenses Outlook

NVIDIA's outlook for Q1 FY27 GAAP operating expenses is expected to be approximately $7.7 billion ($7,700 million), inclusive of $1.9 billion of stock-based compensation expense.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Outlook section and GAAP to Non-GAAP Outlook reconciliation table.

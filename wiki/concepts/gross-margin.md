# Gross Margin

Gross margin is a key profitability metric for NVIDIA. According to the Q4 FY26 investor presentation, GAAP gross margin was 75.0% in Q4 FY26 (up from 73.0% in Q4 FY25), while Non-GAAP gross margin was 75.2% (up from 73.5% in Q4 FY25). The year-on-year increase was driven by lower inventory provisions. The sequential increase was driven by the continued ramp of Blackwell with an improved mix and cost structure. For Q1 FY27, Non-GAAP gross margin is expected to be 75.0% ± 50 bps, with GAAP gross margin of 74.9%.

# Gross Margin

Financial metric representing gross profit as a percentage of revenue. NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 GAAP gross margin was 75.0% (up 2.0 pts YoY) and Non-GAAP gross margin was 75.2% (up 1.7 pts YoY). FY26 GAAP gross margin was 71.1% and Non-GAAP gross margin was 71.3%. Q1 FY27 outlook: GAAP 74.9% and Non-GAAP 75.0%, plus or minus 50 bps.

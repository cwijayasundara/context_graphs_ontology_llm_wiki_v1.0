# Q4 FY26 GAAP Diluted EPS

NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 GAAP diluted earnings per share was $1.76, up 35% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $1.30) and up 98% year-over-year (Q4 FY25: $0.89).

# NVIDIA Non-GAAP Gross Margin Q4 FY26

NVIDIA Non-GAAP Gross Margin was **75.2%** in Q4 FY26, up 1.7 percentage points from 73.5% in Q4 FY25. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, Non-GAAP P&L table and Gross Margin table.

# FY26 GAAP Gross Margin

NVIDIA's FY26 GAAP gross margin was 71.1%, down from 75.0% in FY25 (a decrease of 3.9 percentage points).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Fiscal 2026 GAAP summary table.

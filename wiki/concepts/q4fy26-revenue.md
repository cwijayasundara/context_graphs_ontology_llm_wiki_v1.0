# Q4 FY26 Revenue

NVIDIA's revenue for Q4 FY26 was a record $68,127 million ($68.1 billion), up 73% from a year ago (Q4 FY25: $39,331M) and up 20% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $57,006M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, GAAP and Non-GAAP summary tables.

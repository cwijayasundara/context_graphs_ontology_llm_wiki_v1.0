# Quarterly Revenue Trend

A Quarterly Revenue Trend tracks a company's revenue performance across consecutive fiscal quarters, typically broken down by market segment or product line. It is a key financial metric used to assess growth momentum, seasonality, and market segment contribution.

This concept is exemplified by NVIDIA's revenue trend from Q1 FY25 through Q4 FY26, showing total revenue growth from $26,044M to $68,127M.

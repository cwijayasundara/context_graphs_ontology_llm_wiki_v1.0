# NVIDIA GAAP Operating Expense Dollars Q4 FY26

NVIDIA GAAP Operating Expenses were **$6,794 million** ($6.794 billion) in Q4 FY26, reconciled from Non-GAAP operating expenses of $5,102 million. Adjustments: $90 million acquisition-related costs, $1,564 million stock-based compensation, $38 million other. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, Q4 FY26 reconciliation table (page 12).

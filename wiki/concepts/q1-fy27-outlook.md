# Q1 FY27 Outlook

NVIDIA provided Q1 FY27 financial outlook in its Q4 FY26 investor presentation. Key projections include: Revenue of $78.0 billion ± 2%, Non-GAAP Gross Margin of 75.0% ± 50 bps, and Operating Expenses of approximately $7.5 billion. For the full year fiscal 2027, non-GAAP tax rates are expected to be between 17.0% and 19.0%, excluding any discrete items and material changes to the tax environment. Starting in Q1 FY27, stock-based compensation (SBC) expense will be included in non-GAAP results. The outlook does not assume any Data Center compute revenue from China.

# Q1 FY27 Outlook

NVIDIA's outlook for the first quarter of fiscal 2027. Revenue expected to be $78.0 billion, plus or minus 2%. GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins expected to be 74.9% and 75.0%, respectively. Not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in the outlook.

# Q4 FY26 Non-GAAP Operating Income

NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 Non-GAAP operating income was $46,107 million ($46.1 billion), up 22% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $37,752M) and up 81% year-over-year (Q4 FY25: $25,516M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Non-GAAP summary table.

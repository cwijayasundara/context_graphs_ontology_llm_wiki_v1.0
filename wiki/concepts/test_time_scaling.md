# Test-Time Scaling

Test-time scaling inference is the art of applying more compute during inference to improve accuracy. NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra boosts training and test-time scaling inference to enable organizations everywhere to accelerate applications such as AI reasoning, agentic AI and physical AI.

The GB300 NVL72 rack-scale solution is built specifically for test-time scaling, acting as a single massive GPU that allows AI models to access increased compute capacity to explore different solutions to problems and break down complex requests into multiple steps, resulting in higher-quality responses.

# NVIDIA GAAP Net Income Dollars Q4 FY26

NVIDIA GAAP Net Income was **$42,960 million** ($42.960 billion) in Q4 FY26, reconciled from Non-GAAP net income of $39,552 million. Adjustments: $(138) million acquisition-related costs, $(1,633) million stock-based compensation, $5,441 million other (net gain from equity securities, legal settlements, interest expense), $(262) million tax impact.

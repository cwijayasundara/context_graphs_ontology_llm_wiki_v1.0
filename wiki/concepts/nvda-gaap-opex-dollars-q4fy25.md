# NVIDIA GAAP Operating Expense Dollars Q4 FY25

NVIDIA GAAP Operating Expenses were **$4,689 million** ($4.689 billion) in Q4 FY25, reconciled from Non-GAAP operating expenses of $3,378 million. Adjustments: $43 million acquisition-related costs, $1,268 million stock-based compensation. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, Q4 FY25 reconciliation table (page 13).

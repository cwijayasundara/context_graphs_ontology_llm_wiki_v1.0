# AI Supercomputing

AI supercomputing refers to high-performance computing systems specifically designed for AI workloads such as training and inference. The NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra DGX SuperPOD provides out-of-the-box AI supercomputing for the age of agentic and physical AI, scaling up to tens of thousands of Grace Blackwell Ultra Superchips.

# Data Center Compute Revenue FY26

NVIDIA's Data Center compute revenue for FY26 was $162,361 million, up 59% from FY25 ($102,196M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Revenue by Market Platform table (FY26).

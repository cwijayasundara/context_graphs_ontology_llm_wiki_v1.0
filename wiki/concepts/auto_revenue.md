# Auto Revenue

Auto Revenue represents the portion of NVIDIA's revenue derived from sales of automotive platforms, including the DRIVE platform for autonomous vehicles, AI cockpit solutions, and related software and services for the transportation industry.

Per the Q4 FY26 quarterly revenue trend document, Auto revenue grew steadily from $329M in Q1 FY25 to $604M in Q4 FY26.

Quarterly figures ($ in millions):
- Q4 FY26: $604
- Q3 FY26: $592
- Q2 FY26: $586
- Q1 FY26: $567
- Q4 FY25: $570
- Q3 FY25: $449
- Q2 FY25: $346
- Q1 FY25: $329

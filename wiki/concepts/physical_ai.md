# Physical AI

Physical AI refers to AI systems that interact with and control physical environments, including robotics and smart city solutions. NVIDIA and SDAIA will train government and university scientists and engineers on developing and deploying models for physical AI. Aramco Digital will also create an engineering and robotics center of excellence.

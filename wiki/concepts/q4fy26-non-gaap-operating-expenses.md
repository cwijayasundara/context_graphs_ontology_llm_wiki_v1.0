# Q4 FY26 Non-GAAP Operating Expenses

NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 Non-GAAP operating expenses were $5,102 million, up 51% from a year ago (Q4 FY25: $3,378M) and up 21% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $4,215M). Increases were driven by compute and infrastructure costs and compensation and benefits.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Non-GAAP summary table and Expenses section.

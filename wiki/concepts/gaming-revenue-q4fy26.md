# Gaming Revenue Q4 FY26

NVIDIA's Gaming revenue for Q4 FY26 was $3,727 million ($3.7 billion), up 47% from a year ago (Q4 FY25: $2,544M) but down 13% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $4,265M). Growth was driven by strong Blackwell demand. Sequential decline was due to channel inventory naturally moderating following a season of strong holiday demand. Supply constraints are expected to be a headwind to Gaming in Q1 FY27 and beyond.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Revenue by Market Platform table (Q4).

# AI Reasoning

AI reasoning is the capability of artificial intelligence systems to perform logical inference, problem-solving, and multi-step thinking to arrive at conclusions. The age of AI reasoning demands orders of magnitude more computing performance than traditional AI workloads.

NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra is designed specifically for this moment — it is a single versatile platform that can easily and efficiently do pretraining, post-training and reasoning AI inference. The GB300 NVL72 connects 72 Blackwell Ultra GPUs and 36 Grace CPUs, acting as a single massive GPU built for test-time scaling, allowing AI models to explore different solutions and break down complex requests into multiple steps.

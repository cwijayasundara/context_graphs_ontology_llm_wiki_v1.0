# Compute & Networking Segment Revenue FY26

NVIDIA's Compute & Networking segment revenue for FY26 was $193,479 million, up 67% from FY25 ($116,193M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Revenue by Reportable Segments table (FY26).

# Data Center Revenue Q4 FY26

NVIDIA's Data Center revenue for Q4 FY26 was a record $62,314 million ($62.3 billion), up 75% from a year ago (Q4 FY25: $35,580M) and up 22% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $51,215M). Driven by the major platform shifts - accelerated computing and AI. Hyperscaler revenue remained the largest customer category at slightly over 50% of Data Center revenue.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Revenue by Market Platform table (Q4).

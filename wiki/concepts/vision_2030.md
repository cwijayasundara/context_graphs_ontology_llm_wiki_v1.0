# Saudi Vision 2030

Saudi Vision 2030 is the Kingdom’s strategic framework to reduce dependence on oil, diversify the economy, and develop public service sectors. The NVIDIA partnership aligns with Vision 2030 goals of economic diversification, digital leadership, and building a knowledge-based society and advanced digital economy.

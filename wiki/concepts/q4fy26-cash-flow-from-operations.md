# Q4 FY26 Cash Flow from Operations

NVIDIA's cash flow from operating activities was $36.2 billion in Q4 FY26, up from $16.6 billion a year ago and up from $23.8 billion a quarter ago. The year-on-year and sequential increases reflect growth in revenue.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow section.

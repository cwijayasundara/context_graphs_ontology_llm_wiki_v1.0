# GAAP

Generally Accepted Accounting Principles. NVIDIA reports GAAP financial measures including revenue, gross margin, operating expenses, operating income, net income, and diluted earnings per share. Q4 FY26 GAAP gross margin was 75.0% and FY26 GAAP gross margin was 71.1%.

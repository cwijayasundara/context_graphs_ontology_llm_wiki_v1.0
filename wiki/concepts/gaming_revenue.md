# Gaming Revenue

Gaming Revenue represents the portion of NVIDIA's revenue derived from sales of GeForce GPUs, gaming platforms, and related software and services targeting the consumer gaming market.

Per the Q4 FY26 quarterly revenue trend document, Gaming revenue showed steady performance ranging from $2,544M to $4,287M across the eight reported quarters.

Quarterly figures ($ in millions):
- Q4 FY26: $3,727
- Q3 FY26: $4,265
- Q2 FY26: $4,287
- Q1 FY26: $3,763
- Q4 FY25: $2,544
- Q3 FY25: $3,279
- Q2 FY25: $2,880
- Q1 FY25: $2,647

# Compute & Networking Segment Revenue Q4 FY26

NVIDIA's Compute & Networking segment revenue for Q4 FY26 was $61,651 million ($61.7 billion), up 21% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $50,908M) and up 71% year-over-year (Q4 FY25: $36,036M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Revenue by Reportable Segments table (Q4).

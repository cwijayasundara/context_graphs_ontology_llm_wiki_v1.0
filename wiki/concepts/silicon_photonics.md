# Silicon Photonics

Silicon photonics is the fusion of electronic circuits and optical communications at massive scale. NVIDIA has achieved this integration directly into networking switches, integrating optics innovations with 4x fewer lasers to deliver 3.5x more power efficiency, 63x greater signal integrity, 10x better network resiliency at scale and 1.3x faster deployment compared with traditional methods.

NVIDIA's silicon photonics ecosystem includes TSMC, Browave, Coherent, Corning Incorporated, Fabrinet, Foxconn, Lumentum, SENKO, SPIL, Sumitomo Electric Industries and TFC Communication.

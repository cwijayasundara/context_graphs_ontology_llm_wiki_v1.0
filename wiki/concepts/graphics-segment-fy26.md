# Graphics Segment Revenue FY26

NVIDIA's Graphics segment revenue for FY26 was $22,459 million, up 57% from FY25 ($14,304M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Revenue by Reportable Segments table (FY26).

# Data Center Networking

NVIDIA's Data Center networking revenue. Q4 FY26 revenue was a record $11.0 billion, up 263% YoY and 34% sequentially. FY26 revenue was $31.4 billion, up 142% YoY. Growth driven by introduction and continued ramp of NVLink compute fabric for GB200 and GB300 systems and growth of Ethernet and InfiniBand platforms.

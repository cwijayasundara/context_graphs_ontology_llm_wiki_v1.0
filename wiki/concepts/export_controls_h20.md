# Export Controls / H20 Export License Requirement

On April 9, 2025, NVIDIA was informed by the U.S. government that a license is required for exports of its H20 products into the China market. As a result, NVIDIA incurred a $4.5 billion charge in Q1 FY2026 associated with H20 excess inventory and purchase obligations. Sales of H20 products were $4.6 billion for Q1 FY2026 prior to the new requirements, and NVIDIA was unable to ship an additional $2.5 billion of H20 revenue. The Q2 FY2026 outlook reflects a loss in H20 revenue of approximately $8.0 billion due to the recent export control limitations.

# Inference Serving

Inference serving refers to the deployment and operation of AI models to generate predictions or outputs in production environments. NVIDIA Dynamo is a new AI inference-serving software designed to maximize token revenue generation for AI factories deploying reasoning AI models.

It orchestrates and accelerates inference communication across thousands of GPUs, delivering leaps in throughput while reducing response times and model serving costs.

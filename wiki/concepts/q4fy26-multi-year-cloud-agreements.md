# Q4 FY26 Multi-Year Cloud Service Agreements

NVIDIA's multi-year cloud service agreements were $27.0 billion as of Q4 FY26, up from $26.0 billion sequentially, to support the growing needs of research and development efforts.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow section.

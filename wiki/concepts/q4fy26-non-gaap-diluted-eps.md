# Q4 FY26 Non-GAAP Diluted EPS

NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share was $1.62, up 25% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $1.30) and up 82% year-over-year (Q4 FY25: $0.89).

# OEM & Other Revenue

OEM & Other Revenue represents the portion of NVIDIA's revenue derived from sales to original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) and other miscellaneous revenue streams not categorized under the primary market segments.

Per the Q4 FY26 quarterly revenue trend document, OEM & Other revenue remained a relatively small segment, ranging from $78M to $174M across the eight reported quarters.

Quarterly figures ($ in millions):
- Q4 FY26: $161
- Q3 FY26: $174
- Q2 FY26: $173
- Q1 FY26: $111
- Q4 FY25: $126
- Q3 FY25: $97
- Q2 FY25: $88
- Q1 FY25: $78

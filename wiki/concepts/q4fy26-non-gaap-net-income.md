# Q4 FY26 Non-GAAP Net Income

NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 Non-GAAP net income was $39,552 million ($39.6 billion), up 25% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $31,767M) and up 79% year-over-year (Q4 FY25: $22,066M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Non-GAAP summary table.

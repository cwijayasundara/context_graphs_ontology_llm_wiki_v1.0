# AI Factory

An AI Factory is a data center infrastructure designed specifically to run AI workloads at scale. According to Jensen Huang, "every company will build or rent AI factories to run their businesses and power the intelligence of their products." NVIDIA is accelerating the trillion-dollar IT infrastructure transition to enterprise AI factories with RTX PRO Servers and the NVIDIA Enterprise AI Factory validated design. AI factories are built with GPU-accelerated infrastructure (replacing CPU-based systems) and can run virtually every enterprise workload including AI, design, engineering, and business applications.

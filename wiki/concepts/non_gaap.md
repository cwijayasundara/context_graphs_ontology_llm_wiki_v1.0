# Non-GAAP

NVIDIA's non-GAAP financial measures exclude stock-based compensation expense, acquisition-related and other costs, gains/losses from non-marketable and publicly-held equity securities, interest expense related to amortization of debt discount, and associated tax impacts. Beginning in Q1 FY27, NVIDIA will include stock-based compensation expense in its non-GAAP financial measures. Q4 FY26 Non-GAAP gross margin was 75.2% and FY26 Non-GAAP gross margin was 71.3%.

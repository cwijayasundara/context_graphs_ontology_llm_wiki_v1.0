# Q1 FY2026 Earnings

NVIDIA's Q1 FY2026 earnings report for the quarter ended April 27, 2025. Key financial results include:
- Revenue: $44.1 billion (up 12% Q/Q, up 69% Y/Y)
- GAAP gross margin: 60.5%; Non-GAAP gross margin: 61.0%
- Excluding the $4.5 billion H20 charge, non-GAAP gross margin would have been 71.3%
- GAAP EPS: $0.76; Non-GAAP EPS: $0.81
- Excluding H20 charge, non-GAAP EPS would have been $0.96
- Data Center revenue: $39.1 billion (up 10% Q/Q, up 73% Y/Y)
- Gaming revenue: $3.8 billion (up 48% Q/Q, up 42% Y/Y) - record quarter
- Professional Visualization revenue: $509 million (up 19% Y/Y)
- Automotive revenue: $567 million (up 72% Y/Y)
- Q2 FY2026 revenue outlook: $45.0 billion, plus or minus 2%

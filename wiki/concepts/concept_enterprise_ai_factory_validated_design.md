# NVIDIA Enterprise AI Factory Validated Design

The NVIDIA Enterprise AI Factory validated design is a full-stack, validated reference architecture for building on-premises AI factories. It is based on recommended hardware configurations from NVIDIA Enterprise Reference Architectures and includes RTX PRO Servers, NVIDIA Spectrum-X Ethernet networking, NVIDIA BlueField DPUs, NVIDIA-Certified Storage systems, and NVIDIA AI Enterprise software. The design provides AI software stack recommendations, deployment best practices, and guidance for scaling enterprise AI factories to help organizations meet their growing AI business needs efficiently and reliably.

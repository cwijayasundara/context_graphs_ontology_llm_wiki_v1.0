# AI Factory

AI factories are a new class of data centers with extreme scale, designed to connect millions of GPUs across sites for artificial intelligence workloads. As AI factories grow to unprecedented sizes, networking infrastructure must be reinvented to keep pace. NVIDIA's silicon photonics networking switches aim to enable scaling to a million GPUs and beyond.

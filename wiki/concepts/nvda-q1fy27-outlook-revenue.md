# NVIDIA Q1 FY27 Outlook Revenue

NVIDIA Q1 FY27 (quarter ending April 2026) revenue outlook is **$78.0 billion** (+/- 2%). This outlook does not assume any Data Center compute revenue from China. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, Q1 FY27 Outlook slide (page 10).

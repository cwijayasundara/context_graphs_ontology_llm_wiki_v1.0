# FY26 Free Cash Flow

NVIDIA's FY26 free cash flow was $96,575 million, calculated as GAAP net cash provided by operating activities ($102,718M) less purchases related to property and equipment and intangible assets ($(6,042)M) and principal payments on property and equipment and intangible assets ($(101)M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Reconciliation of GAAP to Non-GAAP Financial Measures table.

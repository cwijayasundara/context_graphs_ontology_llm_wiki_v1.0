# Non-GAAP Financial Measures

NVIDIA uses certain non-GAAP measures in its financial reporting. According to the Q4 FY26 investor presentation, these include non-GAAP gross margin, non-GAAP operating expense, non-GAAP operating income, non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP diluted earnings per share, and free cash flow. The presentation provides detailed reconciliation tables showing the adjustments from Non-GAAP to GAAP figures. Key adjustments include acquisition-related costs, stock-based compensation, and other items. Starting in Q1 FY27, stock-based compensation (SBC) expense will be included in non-GAAP results. SBC is described as a foundational component of NVIDIA's compensation program to attract and retain world-class talent.

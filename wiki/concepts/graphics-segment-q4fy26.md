# Graphics Segment Revenue Q4 FY26

NVIDIA's Graphics segment revenue for Q4 FY26 was $6,476 million ($6.5 billion), up 6% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $6,098M) and up 97% year-over-year (Q4 FY25: $3,295M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Revenue by Reportable Segments table (Q4).

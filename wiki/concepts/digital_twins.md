# Digital Twins

Digital twins are virtual simulations of physical systems used to test and optimize solutions. HUMAIN will deploy Saudi Arabia’s first NVIDIA Omniverse Cloud to simulate and test physical AI solutions with digital twins. These initiatives will help industries such as energy, manufacturing, and logistics develop innovative solutions.

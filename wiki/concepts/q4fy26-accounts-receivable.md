# Q4 FY26 Accounts Receivable

NVIDIA's accounts receivable was $38.5 billion as of Q4 FY26 with 51 days sales outstanding (DSO), down from 53 days sequentially, driven by timing of cash collections.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow section.

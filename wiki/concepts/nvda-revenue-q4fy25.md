# NVIDIA Revenue Q4 FY25

NVIDIA reported revenue of **$39.3 billion** in Q4 FY25 (quarter ended January 2025). This is the year-ago comparison for Q4 FY26, which grew 73% to $68.1 billion. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, GAAP P&L table.

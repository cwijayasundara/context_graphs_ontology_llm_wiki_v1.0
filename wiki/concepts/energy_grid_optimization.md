# Energy Grid Optimization

Energy grid optimization is a focus area of the collaboration between NVIDIA and Tapestry (X's moonshot for the electric grid). They are exploring methods for increasing the speed and accuracy of electric grid simulations, focusing on challenges of integrating new energy sources and expanding grid capacity to meet growing demands of data centers and AI while ensuring grid stability. Potential solutions include using AI to optimize the interconnection process.

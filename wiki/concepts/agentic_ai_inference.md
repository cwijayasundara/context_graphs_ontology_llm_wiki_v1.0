# Agentic AI Inference

Agentic AI inference refers to AI workloads involving autonomous agents that can perform complex tasks, reason, and make decisions. NVIDIA NVLink Fusion enables custom silicon scale-up to meet the requirements of demanding workloads for model training and agentic AI inference, indicating that agentic AI represents a significant and computationally intensive class of AI applications driving infrastructure requirements.

# Data Center Compute Revenue Q4 FY26

NVIDIA's Data Center compute revenue for Q4 FY26 was a record $51,334 million ($51.3 billion), up 58% from a year ago (Q4 FY25: $32,556M) and up 19% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $43,028M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Revenue by Market Platform table (Q4).

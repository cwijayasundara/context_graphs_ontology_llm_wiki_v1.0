# NVIDIA FY27 Non-GAAP Tax Rate Outlook

For the full year fiscal 2027, NVIDIA expects non-GAAP tax rates to be between **17.0% and 19.0%**, excluding any discrete items and material changes to the tax environment. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, Q1 FY27 Outlook slide (page 10).

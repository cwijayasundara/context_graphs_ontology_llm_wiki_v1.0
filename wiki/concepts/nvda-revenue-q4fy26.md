# NVIDIA Revenue Q4 FY26

NVIDIA reported record revenue of **$68.1 billion** in Q4 FY26 (quarter ended January 2026), up 73% year-over-year from $39.3 billion in Q4 FY25. This represents the largest quarter-over-quarter growth in company history. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, GAAP P&L table and Revenue slide.

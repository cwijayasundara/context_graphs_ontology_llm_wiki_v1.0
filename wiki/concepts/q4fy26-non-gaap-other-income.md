# Q4 FY26 Non-GAAP Total Other Income

NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 GAAP total other income, net was $6,098 million. Non-GAAP total other income, net was $620 million after excluding gains from non-marketable equity securities and publicly-held equity securities ($(5,491)M) and other items ($13M).

Net other income for Q4 FY26 was $5.6 billion, driven by unrealized gains in non-marketable and publicly-held equity securities, including gains from the previously announced investment in Intel's common stock.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Reconciliation of GAAP to Non-GAAP Financial Measures table and Other Income & Expense section.

# Robotics

Robotics is a central focus of the NVIDIA-Alphabet-Google collaboration. Key initiatives include: (1) Intrinsic's AI for industrial robotics using NVIDIA Isaac Manipulator foundation models; (2) Development of Newton, an open-source physics engine for robotics simulation, by NVIDIA, Google DeepMind, and Disney Research; (3) Real-time visualization of robot workcells via OpenUSD streaming between Intrinsic Flowstate and NVIDIA Omniverse.

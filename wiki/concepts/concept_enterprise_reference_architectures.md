# NVIDIA Enterprise Reference Architectures

NVIDIA Enterprise Reference Architectures provide recommended hardware configurations that form the foundation of the NVIDIA Enterprise AI Factory validated design. They help partners build Blackwell AI factories featuring NVIDIA-Certified Servers, Spectrum-X networking, BlueField DPUs, NVIDIA-Certified Storage, and NVIDIA AI Enterprise software.

# NVIDIA Free Cash Flow Q4 FY26

NVIDIA Free Cash Flow was **$34.9 billion** in Q4 FY26, up from $15.5 billion in Q4 FY25. The company is investing for growth while returning capital to shareholders. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, Summary Cash Flow Items slide and Free Cash Flow reconciliation table.

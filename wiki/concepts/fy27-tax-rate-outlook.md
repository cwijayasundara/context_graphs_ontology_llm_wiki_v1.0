# FY27 Tax Rate Outlook

For the full year fiscal 2027, NVIDIA expects GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates to be between 17.0% and 19.0%, excluding any discrete items and material changes to the tax environment.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Outlook section.

# Q1 FY27 GAAP Gross Margin Outlook

NVIDIA's outlook for Q1 FY27 GAAP gross margin is expected to be 74.9%, plus or minus 50 basis points, inclusive of a 0.1% impact from stock-based compensation expense.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Outlook section and GAAP to Non-GAAP Outlook reconciliation table.

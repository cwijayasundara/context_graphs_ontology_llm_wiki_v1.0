# NVIDIA Capital Return Q4 FY26

NVIDIA returned **$8.1 billion** to shareholders in Q4 FY26 through dividends and share repurchases, up from $4.1 billion in Q4 FY25. Source: NVDA-F4Q26-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf, Summary Cash Flow Items slide.

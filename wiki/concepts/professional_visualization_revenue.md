# Professional Visualization Revenue

Professional Visualization Revenue represents the portion of NVIDIA's revenue derived from sales of professional graphics solutions, including NVIDIA RTX GPUs and Omniverse platforms, serving industries such as media & entertainment, architecture, engineering, construction, and manufacturing.

Per the Q4 FY26 quarterly revenue trend document, Professional Visualization revenue grew from $427M in Q1 FY25 to $1,321M in Q4 FY26, showing strong acceleration in the later quarters.

Quarterly figures ($ in millions):
- Q4 FY26: $1,321
- Q3 FY26: $760
- Q2 FY26: $601
- Q1 FY26: $509
- Q4 FY25: $511
- Q3 FY25: $486
- Q2 FY25: $454
- Q1 FY25: $427

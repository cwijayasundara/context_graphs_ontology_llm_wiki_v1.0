# Reasoning Models

Reasoning models are AI models designed for complex reasoning tasks. NVIDIA introduced Blackwell Ultra and NVIDIA Dynamo for accelerating and scaling AI reasoning models. The Blackwell NVL72 AI supercomputer is described as a 'thinking machine' designed for reasoning. The Llama Nemotron family of models includes reasoning capabilities.

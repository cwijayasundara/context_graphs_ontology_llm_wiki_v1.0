# Free Cash Flow

Free cash flow is a key liquidity metric for NVIDIA. According to the Q4 FY26 investor presentation, free cash flow was $34.9B in Q4 FY26, up significantly from $15.5B in Q4 FY25. The reconciliation shows Q4 FY26 free cash flow of $34,902M, with purchases of property and equipment and intangible assets of $1,284M, and principal payments on property and equipment and intangible assets of $4M, resulting in net cash provided by operating activities of $36,190M. This compares to Q4 FY25 free cash flow of $15,519M.

# Q4 FY26 Inventory

NVIDIA's inventory was $21.4 billion as of Q4 FY26, up from $19.8 billion sequentially. Total supply-related commitments were $95.2 billion. NVIDIA has strategically secured inventory and capacity to meet demand beyond the next several quarters.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow section.

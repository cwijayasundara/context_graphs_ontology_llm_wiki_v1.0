# Q1 FY27 Revenue Outlook

NVIDIA's outlook for Q1 FY27 revenue is expected to be $78.0 billion, plus or minus 2%. The company is not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in its outlook.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Outlook section.

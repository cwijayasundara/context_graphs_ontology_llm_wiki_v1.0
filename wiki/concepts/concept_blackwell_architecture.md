# NVIDIA Blackwell Architecture

The NVIDIA Blackwell architecture is a GPU architecture that delivers leading performance and energy efficiency for data centers. It powers the NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs and enables enterprises to unlock the full potential of AI in their data center infrastructure. Blackwell AI factories are ideal for accelerating workloads across a broad range of enterprise applications including AI inference, physical AI, design, scientific computing, graphics, and video.

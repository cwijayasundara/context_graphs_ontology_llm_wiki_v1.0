# Data Center Market Platform

NVIDIA's Data Center market platform. Q4 FY26 revenue was a record $62.3 billion, up 75% YoY and 22% sequentially. FY26 revenue was $193.7 billion, up 68% YoY. Includes Compute and Networking sub-segments. Driven by accelerated computing and AI platform shifts. Hyperscaler revenue remained the largest customer category at slightly over 50% of Data Center revenue.

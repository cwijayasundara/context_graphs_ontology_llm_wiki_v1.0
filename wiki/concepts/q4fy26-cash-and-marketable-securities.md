# Q4 FY26 Cash and Marketable Securities

NVIDIA's cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were $62.6 billion as of Q4 FY26 (January 25, 2026), up from $43.2 billion a year ago and $60.6 billion a quarter ago. The increases primarily reflect higher revenue, partially offset by outlays for an intellectual property license, strategic investments and stock repurchases.

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow section.

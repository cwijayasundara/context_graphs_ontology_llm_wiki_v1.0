# Q4 FY26 GAAP Operating Income

NVIDIA's Q4 FY26 GAAP operating income was $44,299 million ($44.3 billion), up 23% sequentially (Q3 FY26: $36,010M) and up 84% year-over-year (Q4 FY25: $24,034M).

Source: CFO Commentary on Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results, GAAP summary table.

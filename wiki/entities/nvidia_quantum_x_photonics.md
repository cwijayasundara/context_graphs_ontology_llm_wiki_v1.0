# NVIDIA Quantum-X Photonics

NVIDIA Quantum-X Photonics InfiniBand switches unveiled at GTC 2025. They provide 144 ports of 800Gb/s InfiniBand based on 200Gb/s SerDes and use a liquid-cooled design to efficiently cool the onboard silicon photonics. They offer 2x faster speeds and 5x higher scalability for AI compute fabrics compared with the previous generation.

Expected availability: later in 2025.

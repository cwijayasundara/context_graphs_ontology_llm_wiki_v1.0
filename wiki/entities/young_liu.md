# Young Liu

Young Liu is the chairman and CEO of Foxconn (Hon Hai Technology Group). In this press release dated May 18, 2025, he stated: "Foxconn builds technology that underpins modern life, and now, we're building computing infrastructure to scale the next generation of breakthroughs across Taiwan. By building this AI factory with NVIDIA and TSMC, we are laying the groundwork to connect people in Taiwan as well as government organizations and enterprises such as TSMC to accelerate innovation and empower industries."

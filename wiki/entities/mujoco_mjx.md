# MuJoCo / MJX

MuJoCo is a physics engine for detailed rigid-body simulation, widely used in robotics research. MJX is MuJoCo's existing GPU-accelerated simulator. Newton is compatible with MuJoCo and will accelerate robotics ML workloads by more than 70x compared with MJX.

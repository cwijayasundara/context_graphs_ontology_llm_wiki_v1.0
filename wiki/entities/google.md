# Google

Google is the largest subsidiary of Alphabet Inc. In this collaboration with NVIDIA, Google is contributing AI models (Gemma, Gemini), frameworks (JAX, MaxText), and AI infrastructure expertise. Google Cloud is among the first to adopt NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 and RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs.

# Alphabet Inc.

Alphabet Inc. is the parent company of Google, Google Cloud, Google DeepMind, Intrinsic, X, and other subsidiaries. In this March 2025 collaboration with NVIDIA, Alphabet companies are working on agentic AI, physical AI, robotics, drug discovery (via Isomorphic Labs), energy grid optimization (via Tapestry), and AI infrastructure. Sundar Pichai is CEO of Google and Alphabet.

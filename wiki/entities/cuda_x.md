# NVIDIA CUDA-X

NVIDIA CUDA-X is a collection of libraries that are part of NVIDIA's ecosystem of powerful development tools. The Blackwell platform builds on CUDA-X libraries, along with over 6 million developers and 4,000+ applications scaling performance across thousands of GPUs.

# NVIDIA ConnectX-8 SuperNICs

NVIDIA ConnectX-8 SuperNICs are part of NVIDIA's end-to-end networking platform that delivers up to 800Gb/s of throughput. They are featured alongside NVIDIA Spectrum-X Ethernet switches and NVIDIA Quantum-X800 InfiniBand in the networking stack supporting NVIDIA NVLink Fusion and AI factories.

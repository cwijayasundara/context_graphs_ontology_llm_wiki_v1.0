# NVIDIA NIM Microservice

NVIDIA NIM is a microservice for deploying AI models. Gemma is available as a highly optimized NVIDIA NIM microservice, harnessing the power of the open-source NVIDIA TensorRT-LLM library for exceptional inference performance.

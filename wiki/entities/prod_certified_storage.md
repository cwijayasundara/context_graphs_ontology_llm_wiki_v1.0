# NVIDIA-Certified Storage

NVIDIA-Certified Storage systems are validated storage solutions from partners including DDN, Dell, HPE, Hitachi Vantara, IBM, NetApp, Nutanix, Pure Storage, VAST Data, and WEKA. They are a component of the NVIDIA Enterprise AI Factory validated design and support a broad range of workloads on RTX PRO Servers.

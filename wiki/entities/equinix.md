# Equinix

Equinix is a data center and colocation provider. It will be the first to offer the new DGX GB300 and DGX B300 systems in its preconfigured liquid- or air-cooled AI-ready data centers located in 45 markets around the world. Equinix will also offer the NVIDIA Instant AI Factory managed service.

# Isomorphic Labs

Isomorphic Labs is a company founded by Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis, focused on reimagining drug discovery with AI. It has built a state-of-the-art drug design engine housed on Google Cloud with NVIDIA GPUs to enable the scale and performance needed to develop groundbreaking AI models that can help advance human health.

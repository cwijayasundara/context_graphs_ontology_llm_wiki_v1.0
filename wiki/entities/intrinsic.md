# Intrinsic

Intrinsic is an Alphabet company focused on making intelligently adaptive AI for robotics usable and valuable for manufacturers across industries. Partnering with NVIDIA, Intrinsic has built deeper developer workflows for Intrinsic Flowstate to support NVIDIA Isaac Manipulator foundation models for universal robot grasping capability. At GTC, Intrinsic shared an early OpenUSD framework streaming connection between Intrinsic Flowstate and NVIDIA Omniverse for real-time visualization of robot workcells.

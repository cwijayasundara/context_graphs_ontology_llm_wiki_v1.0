# NVIDIA RTX PRO Servers

NVIDIA RTX PRO Servers are data center systems built with NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs. They extend the performance and energy efficiency of the NVIDIA Blackwell architecture to data centers, enabling them to run virtually every enterprise workload and driving the shift from CPU-based systems to GPU-accelerated infrastructure. RTX PRO Servers can support up to eight NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs, including NVIDIA BlueField-3 DPUs and NVIDIA ConnectX-8 SuperNICs with built-in PCIe Gen 6 switches. They are part of the NVIDIA Enterprise AI Factory validated design.

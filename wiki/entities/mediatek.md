# MediaTek

MediaTek is a global fabless semiconductor company. It is among the first adopters of NVIDIA NVLink Fusion, leveraging its world-class ASIC design services and deep expertise in high-speed interconnects to collaborate with NVIDIA on building next-generation AI infrastructure. The collaboration began in the automotive segment and now extends to cloud-scale AI.

# NVIDIA Llama Nemotron Reason Models

NVIDIA Llama Nemotron Reason models are a family of open reasoning AI models launched by NVIDIA. Blackwell systems are ideal for running these new models, along with the NVIDIA AI-Q Blueprint, supported in the NVIDIA AI Enterprise software platform.

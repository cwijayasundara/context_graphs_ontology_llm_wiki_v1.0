# Cohere

Cohere is an AI provider that develops large language models. According to the source, Cohere is planning to power agentic AI capabilities in its Command series of models using NVIDIA Dynamo. Saurabh Baji, senior vice president of engineering at Cohere, expects NVIDIA Dynamo will help deliver a premier user experience to enterprise customers.

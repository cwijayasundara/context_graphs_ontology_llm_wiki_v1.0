# NVIDIA Ampere GPU

NVIDIA Ampere is a GPU architecture generation that is approximately six years old as of Q4 FY26. According to the investor presentation, much of the Ampere-based products are sold out in the cloud, demonstrating the longevity and sustained demand for NVIDIA's infrastructure products even across older generations.

# NVIDIA NVLink Fusion

NVIDIA NVLink Fusion is new silicon unveiled by NVIDIA at COMPUTEX 2025 that enables industries to build semi-custom AI infrastructure with the vast ecosystem of partners building with NVIDIA NVLink. It allows custom silicon scale-up to meet demanding workloads for model training and agentic AI inference. Partners including MediaTek, Marvell, Alchip, Astera Labs, Synopsys and Cadence are among the first adopters. Fujitsu and Qualcomm Technologies can also integrate their custom CPUs with NVIDIA GPUs using NVLink Fusion. The silicon design services and solutions are available now.

# NVIDIA AI Enterprise

NVIDIA AI Enterprise is a software platform for building and deploying enterprise-grade AI agents. It includes NVIDIA NIM microservices, NVIDIA Llama Nemotron open reasoning model family, and NVIDIA AI Blueprints frameworks, libraries, and tools.

# HUMAIN

HUMAIN is a subsidiary of Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund focused on AI. It is making a major investment to build AI factories in KSA with a projected capacity of up to 500 megawatts powered by several hundred thousand of NVIDIA’s most advanced GPUs over the next five years. The first phase includes an 18,000 NVIDIA GB300 Grace Blackwell AI supercomputer with NVIDIA InfiniBand networking. HUMAIN will also deploy the country’s first NVIDIA Omniverse Cloud for digital twins.

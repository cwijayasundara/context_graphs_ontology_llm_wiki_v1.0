# Dr. C.C. Wei

Dr. C.C. Wei is the chairman and CEO of TSMC. In this press release dated May 18, 2025, he stated: "At TSMC, innovation lies at the heart of everything we do. By harnessing advanced AI infrastructure, we empower our researchers to accelerate breakthroughs in semiconductor technology, enabling next-generation solutions for our customers and the world. Leveraging this AI factory reinforces our commitment to pushing the limits of AI-driven innovation."

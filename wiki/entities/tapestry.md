# Tapestry

Tapestry is X's moonshot for the electric grid, building AI-powered products for a greener and more reliable future grid. Tapestry and NVIDIA are exploring methods for increasing the speed and accuracy of electric grid simulations, focusing on challenges of integrating new energy sources and expanding grid capacity to meet growing demands of data centers and AI while ensuring grid stability.

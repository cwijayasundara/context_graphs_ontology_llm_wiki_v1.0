# NVIDIA GB300 NVL72

NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 is a compute-dense rack-scale system part of the fifth-generation NVIDIA NVLink platform. It provides a total bandwidth of 1.8 TB/s per GPU — 14x faster than PCIe Gen5. It represents the next generation of NVIDIA's full-rack AI infrastructure solutions.

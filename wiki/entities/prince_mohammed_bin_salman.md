# Prince Mohammed bin Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud

His Royal Highness Prince Mohammed bin Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud is the Crown Prince and Prime Minister of Saudi Arabia. He participated in the state visit with U.S. President Donald Trump during which the NVIDIA-Saudi Arabia AI partnership was announced.

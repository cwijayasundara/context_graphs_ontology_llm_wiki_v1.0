NVIDIA Quantum-X800 InfiniBand is a high-performance networking solution from NVIDIA, available soon. It is part of NVIDIA's end-to-end networking platform delivering up to 800Gb/s of throughput, supporting AI factories built with NVIDIA NVLink Fusion.

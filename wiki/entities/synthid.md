# SynthID

SynthID is a Google DeepMind AI technology that embeds digital watermarks directly into AI-generated images, audio, text or video. NVIDIA will be the first external user of SynthID, using it to preserve the integrity of outputs from NVIDIA Cosmos world foundation models without compromising video quality.

# NVIDIA Cosmos

NVIDIA Cosmos is a world foundation model platform. NVIDIA will use Google DeepMind's SynthID to embed digital watermarks directly into AI-generated outputs from NVIDIA Cosmos world foundation models, available on build.nvidia.com, helping safeguard against misinformation and misattribution without compromising video quality.

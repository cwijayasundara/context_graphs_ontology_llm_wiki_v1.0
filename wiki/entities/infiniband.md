# InfiniBand

InfiniBand is NVIDIA's high-performance networking technology used in data centers and HPC environments. The Q4 FY26 presentation highlights strong adoption of InfiniBand, along with NVLink and Spectrum-X. NVIDIA is described as the world's largest networking business.

# Dr. Abdullah bin Sharaf Alghamdi

H.E. Dr. Abdullah bin Sharaf Alghamdi is the president of the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA). He stated that the partnership with NVIDIA reflects SDAIA’s commitment to harnessing and advancing the potential of data and AI through continuous innovation, aligned with Saudi Vision 2030.

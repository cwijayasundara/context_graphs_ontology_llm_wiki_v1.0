# NVIDIA Omniverse

NVIDIA Omniverse is a platform for simulation and collaboration. In this collaboration, teams from Google DeepMind, Isomorphic Labs, Intrinsic, and X's Tapestry use NVIDIA Omniverse, along with NVIDIA Cosmos and NVIDIA Isaac platforms, to develop robots with grasping skills, reimagine drug discovery, and optimize energy grids. Intrinsic demonstrated an early OpenUSD framework streaming connection between Intrinsic Flowstate and NVIDIA Omniverse for real-time visualization of robot workcells.

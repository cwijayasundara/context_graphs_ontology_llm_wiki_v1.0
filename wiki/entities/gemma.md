# Gemma / Gemma 3

Gemma is Google's family of lightweight, open models. Google DeepMind and NVIDIA partnered to optimize Gemma to run on NVIDIA GPUs. The recent launch of Gemma 3 marks a significant leap forward for open innovation. Gemma is available as a highly optimized NVIDIA NIM microservice using NVIDIA TensorRT-LLM.

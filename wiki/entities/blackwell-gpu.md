# NVIDIA Blackwell GPU

NVIDIA Blackwell is a next-generation GPU architecture. According to the Q4 FY26 investor presentation, Blackwell showed sustained strength with continued ramp contributing to gross margin expansion. The sequential increase in gross margin was driven by the continued ramp of Blackwell with an improved mix and cost structure. Blackwell is a key growth driver for NVIDIA's Data Center compute revenue, which reached $51.3B in Q4 FY26.

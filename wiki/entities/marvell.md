# Marvell

Marvell Technology is a semiconductor company collaborating with NVIDIA to redefine AI factory integration. Marvell custom silicon with NVLink Fusion provides customers a flexible, high-performance foundation for advanced AI infrastructure, delivering the bandwidth, reliability and agility required for next-generation trillion-parameter AI models.

# NVIDIA Blackwell / Blackwell Ultra

NVIDIA Blackwell is a GPU architecture introduced a year prior to this press release. Blackwell Ultra includes the NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 rack-scale solution and the NVIDIA HGX B300 NVL16 system. The GB300 NVL72 delivers 1.5x more AI performance than the GB200 NVL72. Google Cloud offers A4 VMs (B200-based) and A4X VMs (GB200-based) powered by Blackwell GPUs, with A4 generally available and A4X coming soon.

# NVIDIA Blackwell / NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra / NVIDIA GB300 NVL72

NVIDIA Blackwell is NVIDIA's next-generation AI computing architecture. In this press release dated May 18, 2025, the AI factory being built by Foxconn's subsidiary Big Innovation Company will feature 10,000 NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs and NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra systems, including the NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 rack-scale solution. The system will deliver state-of-the-art NVIDIA Blackwell infrastructure to researchers, startups, and industries in Taiwan.

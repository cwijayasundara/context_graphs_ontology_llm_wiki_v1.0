# NVIDIA Corporation

NVIDIA Corporation (NASDAQ: NVDA) is the world leader in accelerated computing. In this press release, NVIDIA announces expanded collaboration with Alphabet and Google across agentic AI, physical AI, robotics, drug discovery, and AI infrastructure. NVIDIA will be the first external user of Google DeepMind's SynthID watermarking technology and is collaborating on open-source physics engine Newton, NVIDIA Isaac platforms, and Blackwell GPU deployments on Google Cloud.

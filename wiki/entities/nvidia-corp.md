# NVIDIA Corporation

NVIDIA Corporation is a leading technology company specializing in GPU-accelerated computing and AI infrastructure. According to the Q4 FY26 investor presentation, NVIDIA reported record quarterly revenue of $68.1B (+73% YoY), with Data Center revenue up nearly 13x since the emergence of ChatGPT. The company is described as the world's largest networking business and the most performant, lowest cost/token inference provider with the largest installed base. NVIDIA infrastructure is in high demand, with even Hopper and much of the six-year-old Ampere-based products sold out in the cloud.

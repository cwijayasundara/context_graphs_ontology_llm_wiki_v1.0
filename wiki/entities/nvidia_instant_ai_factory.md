# NVIDIA Instant AI Factory

NVIDIA Instant AI Factory is a managed service featuring the Blackwell Ultra-powered NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD with NVIDIA Mission Control software. Equinix will be the first to offer this service in its preconfigured liquid- or air-cooled AI-ready data centers located in 45 markets around the world. It provides fully provisioned, intelligence-generating AI factories optimized for state-of-the-art model training and real-time reasoning workloads.

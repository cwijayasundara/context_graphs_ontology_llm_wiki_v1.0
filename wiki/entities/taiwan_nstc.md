# Taiwan National Science and Technology Council

The Taiwan National Science and Technology Council is a government organization. In this press release, Minister Wu Cheng-Wen announced that the Council will invest in the Big Innovation Company supercomputer to provide AI cloud computing resources to the Taiwan technology ecosystem, accelerating AI development and adoption across sectors. The Council's plan is to create an AI-focused industrial ecosystem in southern Taiwan with a vision of creating a smart AI island filled with smart cities.

# NVIDIA Isaac

NVIDIA Isaac is a platform for robotics development. Teams from Google DeepMind, Isomorphic Labs, Intrinsic, and X's Tapestry use the NVIDIA Isaac platform alongside NVIDIA Omniverse and NVIDIA Cosmos for their collaborations. Intrinsic Flowstate supports NVIDIA Isaac Manipulator foundation models for universal robot grasping capability.

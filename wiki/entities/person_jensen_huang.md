Jensen Huang is the founder and CEO of NVIDIA. In this press release, he stated: "AI is revolutionizing every industry — every company will build or rent AI factories to run their businesses and power the intelligence of their products. With our global partner ecosystem, we’re helping enterprises infuse AI into their workforce, automate their factories and build AI-native products."

# NVIDIA Spectrum-X Photonics

NVIDIA Spectrum-X Photonics Ethernet networking switches unveiled at GTC 2025. They integrate silicon photonics directly into switches to enable AI factories to connect millions of GPUs across sites while drastically reducing energy consumption and operational costs.

Key specifications:
- 1.6 Terabits per second per port
- 3.5x energy savings
- 10x resilience in AI factories
- 4x fewer lasers than traditional methods
- 63x greater signal integrity
- 1.3x faster deployment

Configurations include:
- 128 ports of 800Gb/s or 512 ports of 200Gb/s, delivering 100Tb/s total bandwidth
- 512 ports of 800Gb/s or 2,048 ports of 200Gb/s, for a total throughput of 400Tb/s

Expected availability: 2026 from leading infrastructure and system vendors.

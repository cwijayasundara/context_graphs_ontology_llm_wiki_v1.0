# Vivek Mahajan

Vivek Mahajan is the CTO at Fujitsu. Regarding NVIDIA NVLink Fusion, he said: "Combining Fujitsu's advanced CPU technology with NVIDIA's full-stack AI infrastructure delivers new levels of performance. Fujitsu's next-generation processor, FUJITSU-MONAKA, is a 2-nanometer, Arm-based CPU aiming to achieve extreme power efficiency. Directly connecting our technologies to NVIDIA's architecture marks a monumental step forward in our vision to drive the evolution of AI through world-leading computing technology — paving the way for a new class of scalable, sovereign and sustainable AI systems."

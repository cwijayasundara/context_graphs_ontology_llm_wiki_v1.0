# NVIDIA DGX B300

NVIDIA DGX B300 is an air-cooled AI infrastructure platform designed to bring energy-efficient generative AI and AI reasoning to every data center. It harnesses the NVIDIA B300 NVL16 architecture and is accelerated by NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra GPUs. DGX B300 systems deliver 11x faster AI performance for inference and a 4x speedup for training compared with the Hopper generation. Each system provides 2.3TB of HBM3e memory and includes eight NVIDIA ConnectX-8 SuperNICs and two NVIDIA BlueField-3 DPUs.

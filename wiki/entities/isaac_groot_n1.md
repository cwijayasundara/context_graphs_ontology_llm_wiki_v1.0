# NVIDIA Isaac GR00T N1 / N1.5

NVIDIA Isaac GR00T N1 is the world's first open humanoid robot foundation model, followed by NVIDIA Isaac GR00T N1.5. NVIDIA also announced Isaac GR00T-Dreams, a blueprint for generating synthetic motion data, and NVIDIA Blackwell systems to accelerate humanoid robot development.

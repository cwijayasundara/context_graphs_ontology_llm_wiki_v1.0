# NVIDIA Hopper GPU

NVIDIA Hopper is a GPU architecture generation preceding Blackwell. According to the Q4 FY26 investor presentation, Hopper-based products remain in high demand and are sold out in the cloud. The presentation notes that even Hopper and much of the six-year-old Ampere-based products are sold out, demonstrating sustained demand across multiple product generations.

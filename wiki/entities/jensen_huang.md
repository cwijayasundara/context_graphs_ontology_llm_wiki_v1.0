# Jensen Huang

Jensen Huang is the founder and CEO of NVIDIA. During a state visit with U.S. President Donald Trump and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, Huang stated that the partnership will harness sovereign AI infrastructure and expertise to propel Saudi Arabia to the ranks of global hyperscale AI leaders.

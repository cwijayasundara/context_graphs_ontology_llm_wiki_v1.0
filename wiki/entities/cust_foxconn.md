# Foxconn

Foxconn (Hon Hai Precision Industry) is a global electronics manufacturer and data center system partner. The company is building an AI factory to advance global semiconductor and electronics manufacturing using NVIDIA-accelerated IT infrastructure, as well as its smart electric vehicles, factory digital twins, healthcare, and robotics applications. Foxconn is also a data center system partner offering NVIDIA RTX PRO Servers. Young Liu serves as chairman of Foxconn.

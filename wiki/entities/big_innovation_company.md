# Big Innovation Company

Big Innovation Company is a subsidiary of Foxconn (Hon Hai Technology Group). It is building an NVIDIA Blackwell supercomputer with 10,000 NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs as an NVIDIA Cloud Partner. The Big Innovation Cloud AI factory will feature NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra systems, including the NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 rack-scale solution with NVIDIA NVLink, NVIDIA Quantum InfiniBand, and NVIDIA Spectrum-X Ethernet networking. Big Innovation Cloud plans to participate in the NVIDIA DGX Cloud Lepton marketplace.

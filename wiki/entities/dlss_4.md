# NVIDIA DLSS 4

NVIDIA DLSS 4 is a technology now available in over 125 games, including Black Myth Wukong, DOOM: The Dark Ages, Indiana Jones and the Great Circle, Marvel Rivals, and Star Wars Outlaws. It is also used for AI-powered gaming on the Nintendo Switch 2.

# Google DeepMind

Google DeepMind is Alphabet's AI research division, led by CEO Demis Hassabis. In this collaboration, DeepMind is working with NVIDIA on: (1) SynthID - an AI watermarking technology that NVIDIA will be the first external user of; (2) Optimizing Gemma open models for NVIDIA GPUs; (3) Developing Newton, an open-source physics engine for robotics simulation, in collaboration with Disney Research and NVIDIA; (4) Optimizing Gemini-based workloads on NVIDIA accelerated computing via Vertex AI.

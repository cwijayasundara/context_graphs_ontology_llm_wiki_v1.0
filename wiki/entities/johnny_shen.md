# Johnny Shen

Johnny Shen is the CEO of Alchip Technologies. Regarding NVIDIA NVLink Fusion, he said: "Alchip is supporting adoption of NVLink Fusion by broadening its availability through a design and manufacturing ecosystem, encompassing advanced processes and proven packaging and supported by the ASIC industry's most flexible engagement. It's our contribution to ensuring that the next generation of AI models can be trained and deployed efficiently to meet the demands of tomorrow's intelligent applications."

# Together AI

Together AI is an AI Acceleration Cloud company. According to the source, Together AI is looking to integrate its proprietary Together Inference Engine with NVIDIA Dynamo to enable seamless scaling of inference workloads across GPU nodes. Ce Zhang, chief technology officer of Together AI, highlighted the importance of disaggregated serving and context-aware routing for scaling reasoning models cost-effectively.

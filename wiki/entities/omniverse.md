# NVIDIA Omniverse

NVIDIA Omniverse is a platform described as a physical AI operating system that is being integrated by leading industrial software and service providers (Accenture, Ansys, Databricks, SAP, Schneider Electric with ETAP, and Siemens) into their solutions to accelerate industrial digitalization with physical AI. It is also used in the collaboration with General Motors for next-generation vehicles, factories, and robots.

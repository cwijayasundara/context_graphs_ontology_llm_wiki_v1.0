# Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA)

The Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) is partnering with NVIDIA to deploy up to 5,000 Blackwell GPUs for a sovereign AI factory and enable smart city solutions. SDAIA will also train government and university scientists and engineers on developing and deploying models for physical and agentic AI.
